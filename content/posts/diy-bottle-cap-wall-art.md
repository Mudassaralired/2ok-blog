Bottle cap wall art turns saved caps into a real statement piece for almost nothing. Each cap is a tiny tile: a few hundred make a mandala, a map or a portrait. Every project below is wall-only, with a cost, time and difficulty rating.

> **Key Takeaways**
> - Every project here costs $6 to $45, and most take under four hours.
> - Metal caps need epoxy or E6000; hot glue only holds light plastic caps.
> - Sort caps by colour and size before gluing, or the pattern drifts.
> - Most pieces weigh 1 to 4 kg: check the hanging table before you drill.
> - Seal pieces against rust and fading, or bright caps dull within a year.

## Mosaic and Pattern Projects

### 1. Colourful mosaic mandala

Glue caps in rings on a 60 cm disc: gold center, red, teal, cream, green rings. Cost: $8 to $18. Time: 3 to 4 hours. Difficulty: moderate. 200 caps, 2 kg: two 3 kg hooks. Plan it with the [DIY wall decor guide](/blog/diy-wall-decor-guide).

### 2. Ombre fade panel

Arrange caps in a fade on a 50 by 70 cm board: navy at the bottom, teal and cream in the middle, pale gold at the top. Cost: $6 to $14. Time: 2 to 3 hours. Difficulty: easy. 180 caps, 1.5 kg: one 3 kg hook holds it.

### 3. Geometric grid panel

Build diamonds and triangles from gold, navy, cream and rust-red caps on a dark board, rows kept straight with a pencil grid. Cost: $10 to $20. Time: 3 to 4 hours. Difficulty: moderate. 220 caps, 2.5 kg: two 3 kg hooks.

### 4. World map wall art

Trace a world map on a 70 by 90 cm board: continents in one cap colour, oceans in another, coastlines in gold cord. Cost: $12 to $25. Time: 4 to 5 hours. Difficulty: hard. 300 caps, 3 kg: two hooks, 5 kg anchor.

## Picture and Portrait Projects

Shrink a photo to a 20 by 25 grid, match each square to the nearest cap colour, glue caps row by row. Cost: $15 to $30. Time: 6 to 8 hours. Difficulty: hard. 500 caps, 4 kg: two hooks, 5 kg anchor.

### 6. Sunflower plaque

Make a sunflower on a round 45 cm board: brown center, yellow and gold petals, two green leaves. Cost: $8 to $15. Time: 2 to 3 hours. Difficulty: easy. 120 caps, 1 kg: one 3 kg hook holds it.

## Sorting Bottle Caps by Colour and Size First

Never glue from a mixed jar: sorting first makes the difference between a clean pattern and a muddy one. Wash caps in warm soapy water, dry them fully, and throw out any with rust, dents or bent edges. Sort by colour into bowls: reds, golds, greens, blues, silvers, blacks and whites; count each colour so you know if you have enough gold caps. Sort by size too: crown caps are 32 mm, plastic caps smaller, so mixing them in one row leaves gaps. Lay the pattern dry first, then photograph it.

## Metal vs Plastic Caps: Which Glue to Use

Metal and plastic caps need different glues: hot glue grabs fast but peels off smooth metal, and a heavy mandala sheds caps within months. Use E6000 or two-part epoxy for metal caps: both bond metal to wood and stay flexible. Hot glue suits light plastic caps: plastic is light and the glue holds its rough surface. Super glue suits single repairs only: it turns brittle and snaps.

| Glue | Best for | Hold | Cure time | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hot glue | Light plastic caps | Medium | Seconds | $4 to $8 |
| E6000 | Metal caps, most panels | Strong | 24 hours | $6 to $9 |
| Two-part epoxy | Heavy metal pieces | Very strong | 24 hours | $8 to $12 |
| Construction adhesive | Large boards | Very strong | 1-2 days | $7 to $10 |
| Super glue | Single cap repairs | Medium | Seconds | $3 to $5 |

Test glue first: glue five caps to scrap board, cure fully, hang it upside down overnight. If a cap falls, switch glue. Work ventilated, wear gloves.

## Flattening and Bending Caps Safely

Flattened caps read as smooth tiles; whole caps give 3D texture. Flatten a cap crown-side down on a steel plate with light mallet taps. Never hold a cap with your fingers while hammering: the crimped edge is sharp and slips. Wear safety glasses: a cap can ping off the plate. Flatten metal caps only; plastic caps crack, so use them whole. Bend a cap into a petal with pliers in small steps; stop if paint flakes.

## Backing Boards Compared

The board carries the whole weight: a weak board sags, a heavy board doubles the load. Plywood is the safe default; foam board suits light pieces only. Paint first: dark backgrounds hide gaps, light ones suit pale caps. Add a D-ring before gluing: drilling after risks cracking caps.

## Hanging Bottle Cap Art by Weight

Cap art is heavier than it looks: one cap weighs 2 g, so 200 caps add 400 g, and a 500-cap portrait weighs 4 kg. Hang by the finished weight, not the cap count. Under 1 kg covers the letter and the crab: one 1 kg hook, or a small adhesive hook, holds them. From 1 to 2 kg covers the ombre panel, sunflower and bar sign: one 3 kg hook in a stud or wall plug, never a bare nail. From 2 to 4 kg covers the mandala, map, tree and portrait: two 3 kg hooks 20 to 30 cm apart, a wire across the back, plus a 5 kg anchor over 3 kg.

| Piece weight | Hardware | Hooks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Under 1 kg | 1 kg hook | 1 |
| 1 to 2 kg | 3 kg hook, wall plug | 1 |
| 2 to 3 kg | Two 3 kg hooks, wire back | 2 |
| Over 3 kg | Two hooks, 5 kg anchor | 2 |

Renters: favour adhesive hooks and light pieces, which come down cleanly. Never hang a portrait above a bed with a bare nail; check hooks every six months.

## Sealing Against Rust and Fading

An unsealed piece dulls within a year: chipped caps rust, bright colours fade in sun. Seal with two thin coats of clear spray, holding the can 25 cm away so it mists evenly. Let the first coat dry, then seal the back too against damp walls. Keep cap art out of steamy rooms: humidity rusts crimped edges. Dust monthly with a soft brush.

## Mistakes to Avoid

Hot glue on metal caps is the biggest mistake: it peels off and drops caps within months. Skipping the sort leaves a pattern short of one colour. A single bare nail in drywall works loose and drops a heavy piece. Fingers near the hammer while flattening: the crimped edge is sharp. Skipping the sealer lets caps fade and rust within a year. Mixing metal and plastic caps in one row leaves gaps.

## FAQ

### How many bottle caps do I need for wall art?

A small piece needs 70 to 90 caps, a medium panel needs 180 to 250, and a portrait needs about 500. Count first, and collect 10 percent extra.

### What glue works best for metal bottle caps?

E6000 or two-part epoxy: both bond metal to wood and stay flexible. Hot glue holds light plastic caps only, and peels off metal.

### How do I hang heavy bottle cap wall art?

Use two 3 kg hooks, 20 to 30 cm apart, with a wire across the back, plus a 5 kg anchor over 3 kg. Never hang heavy pieces on a single bare nail.

### How do I stop bottle caps from rusting?

Wash and dry caps fully, throw out any with rust or bent edges, and seal with two thin coats of clear spray. Keep cap art out of steamy rooms.

### Can I use plastic bottle caps for wall art?

Yes, plastic caps suit light pieces like the crab and the letter, and hot glue holds them fine. Use them whole, never flattened: plastic cracks; keep plastic and metal in separate rows.
