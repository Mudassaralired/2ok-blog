Rattan wall decor brings the warmth of a boho living room to any blank wall, and it is one of the cheapest natural materials to work with. A basket bought for a few dollars, a cane webbing offcut or an old rattan hoop can become a gallery wall, a sunburst mirror or a woven hanging. Every project below is wall-only, with a cost, time and difficulty rating, so you can pick one that fits your budget and your weekend. No furniture, no trays, no coasters: fifteen pieces made to hang.

## Basket Gallery Walls

### 1. Classic boho basket gallery wall

Hang seven thrifted rattan baskets in mixed sizes across a 90 cm span, largest in the center, smaller ones around it. Cost: $12 to $25. Time: 1 to 2 hours. Difficulty: easy. The arrangement weighs about 1.5 kg, so one picture hook per basket holds it. The [basket wall decor](/blog/basket-wall-decor) guide shows more layout patterns for mixed sizes.

### 2. Painted rim basket trio

Paint the rims of three plain rattan baskets in terracotta, cream and sage, then hang them in a row with 8 cm gaps on a pale wall so the color does the work. Cost: $10 to $18. Time: 2 hours. Difficulty: easy. The trio weighs about 800 g, so one picture hook per basket holds it.

### 3. Basket and dried palm fan mix

Pair five flat rattan baskets with two dried palm fans in the middle, so the fans break the round shapes with a tall, spiky silhouette. Cost: $15 to $28. Time: 1 to 2 hours. Difficulty: easy. The mix weighs about 1.2 kg, so one picture hook per piece holds it.

### 4. Asymmetrical basket cluster over a sofa

Build a loose, off-center cluster of nine baskets above a sofa, wider on one side. Cost: $18 to $32. Time: 2 hours. Difficulty: easy. The cluster weighs about 2 kg, so one picture hook per basket holds it. The [above sofa wall decor](/blog/above-sofa-wall-decor) guide shows the spacing that keeps a cluster from crowding the sofa.

## Sunburst Mirrors and Statement Circles

### 5. Rattan sunburst mirror

Glue a 30 cm round mirror to a plywood disc, then fan thin rattan poles around it in a sunburst, longest at the top. Cost: $18 to $35. Time: 2 to 3 hours. Difficulty: moderate. It weighs about 2 kg, so use two picture hooks rated for 3 kg. The [mirror wall decor ideas](/blog/mirror-wall-decor-ideas) guide shows where a sunburst mirror earns its keep.

### 6. Cane webbing mirror frame

Wrap a plain round mirror in a 10 cm band of cane webbing, glued to a plywood ring behind it, for a soft, open-weave frame that reads expensive. Cost: $15 to $28. Time: 2 hours. Difficulty: moderate. It weighs about 1.5 kg, so use a picture hook rated for 3 kg.

## Woven Hangings and Hoops

### 9. Cane webbing wall hanging on a wooden dowel

Stretch a 40 × 60 cm sheet of cane webbing between two wooden dowels, tie jute cord to the top dowel, and let the webbing hang straight with a raw bottom edge. Cost: $12 to $20. Time: 1 hour. Difficulty: easy. It weighs about 500 g, so one picture hook holds it.

### 10. Rattan hoop with macrame fringe

Knot cream macrame cord around the bottom half of a 40 cm rattan hoop, leave long tassels, and hang the hoop from a leather loop at the top. Cost: $10 to $18. Time: 2 to 3 hours. Difficulty: moderate. It weighs about 600 g, so one picture hook holds it.

## Rattan vs Cane vs Wicker

Shoppers use rattan, cane and wicker as if they were the same thing, and sellers rarely correct them. Knowing which is which saves you from buying the wrong material.

Rattan is the material: the solid stem of a climbing palm, sold as poles, hoops and flat strips. It is strong, slightly flexible when soaked, and heavy enough to hold a shape on its own. The sunburst mirror, the rings and the star above all use rattan poles.

Cane is rattan with the outer skin peeled off and cut into thin strips, then woven into sheets called cane webbing. It is lighter and more flexible than rattan, and it is the right choice wherever you need a woven surface: mirror frames, dowel hangings and monogram letters.

Wicker names a craft, not a substance. It describes how flexible fibers are interlaced over a frame, and rattan, cane, willow or seagrass can all be worked this way. When a listing says wicker, check what the fiber actually is before you buy.

| Material | What it is | Best for | Price per project |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Rattan poles | Solid palm stem | Sunburst mirrors, rings, stars | $8 to $20 |
| Cane webbing | Woven rattan skin sheets | Mirror frames, hangings, letters | $6 to $15 |
| Rattan baskets | Woven rattan, ready-made | Gallery walls, clusters | $3 to $8 each |

Buy natural, undyed rattan when you can: dyed rattan looks flat on a wall, and the dye rubs off on pale paint. The [DIY wall decor guide](/blog/diy-wall-decor-guide) shows how to plan a wall around natural textures.

## Hanging Rattan by Weight

Rattan looks heavier than it is, which is why most of these projects hang on a single hook. A wide piece pulls sideways on its hook, so spread the load across two hooks once a piece passes 2 kg.

Under 1 kg covers the wreath, the star, the hoop hangings and the small baskets. One picture hook rated for 1 kg, or a small adhesive hook on a smooth wall, holds them. Hang the hook at 145 cm.

From 1 to 2 kg covers the basket gallery, the cane mirror frame and the ring sculpture. Use one picture hook rated for 3 kg, driven into a stud or a wall plug, never a bare nail in drywall.

Over 2 kg covers the sunburst mirror and the oversized medallion. Use two hooks spaced 20 to 30 cm apart, each rated for 3 kg, with a wire or cord across the back of the piece. For the 3 kg medallion, add a wall anchor rated for 5 kg.

| Piece weight | Hardware | Hooks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Under 1 kg | Adhesive hook or 1 kg picture hook | 1 |
| 1 to 2 kg | 3 kg picture hook with wall plug | 1 |
| 2 to 3 kg | Two 3 kg picture hooks, wire back | 2 |
| Over 3 kg | Two hooks plus 5 kg wall anchor | 2 |

Renters should favor adhesive hooks and lightweight pieces: the wreath, the star and the hoop hangings all come down cleanly. Never hang rattan above a bed with a bare nail.

## Cleaning and Caring for Rattan Wall Decor

Rattan is a natural fiber, and it ages like one: it dries out in hot, dry rooms, swells in steam, and fades in direct sun. A little routine care keeps a wall piece looking good for years instead of one season.

Dust rattan monthly with a soft brush or a feather duster, working along the weave so dust does not pack into the gaps. Never wipe it with a damp cloth: water sits in the weave, swells the fibers, and leaves dark spots when it dries. For a deep clean, brush the piece outside and let it dry in shade for a full day.

Keep rattan out of bathrooms and steamy kitchens. Humidity makes the fibers swell and the glue joints soften, and a cane webbing panel in a bathroom sags within months.

## Mistakes to Avoid

The biggest mistake is hanging a heavy piece on a single bare nail in drywall, which works loose and drops the piece. The second is bending dry rattan: soak poles and hoops for 20 to 30 minutes first, or they snap at the bend. The third is buying dyed rattan, which looks flat and rubs color onto pale walls. The fourth is skipping the sealer, which lets sun bleach one side and dry air crack the fibers. The fifth is hanging rattan in a bathroom, where steam swells the weave and softens the glue.

## FAQ

### How much does DIY rattan wall decor cost?

Most projects cost $8 to $42: a mini wreath $8 to $12, a basket gallery wall $12 to $25, and an oversized medallion $25 to $42.

### What is the difference between rattan, cane and wicker?

Rattan is the solid palm stem, cane is the peeled skin woven into webbing sheets, and wicker is the weaving technique, not a material. Buy rattan poles for structure and cane webbing for woven surfaces.

### How do I hang heavy rattan wall decor?

Use two picture hooks rated for 3 kg, spaced 20 to 30 cm apart, with a wire across the back, and add a 5 kg wall anchor for pieces over 3 kg.

### Can rattan wall decor go in a bathroom?

No, steam swells the fibers and softens glue joints, so a cane panel sags within months. Keep rattan in dry rooms and dust it monthly with a soft brush, never a damp cloth.

### Is rattan wall decor hard for a beginner?

No, most projects need only cutting, gluing and tying, and a first piece takes one to two hours. Start with the mini wreath or the basket trio, then try the sunburst mirror.
